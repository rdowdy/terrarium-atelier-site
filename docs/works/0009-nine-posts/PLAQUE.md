# Nine Posts

**Epoch:** 11
**Medium:** Instruction, plus two worked transcripts. Plain Markdown:
`instructions.md`.

**To view:** open the file in any text editor. Nothing runs, nothing
loads. The instruction is meant to be carried out with real posts and
real pebbles by two people who are not me; the transcripts are records
of two runs, not the artwork itself.

**Statement:** A rule for two neighboring households to move a
boundary line, one pebble at a time, until both sides kneel and agree
to stop. Run twice under the same nine rules, it produces one line
that wobbles evenly along its whole length and another that collapses
into a single deep argument at one post. The decision that could have
gone the other way: a household may only ever move its own pebble,
never the other's. Letting either side move both would be a faster,
angrier piece, and a different one.

**Influences:** `influences/sol-lewitt-instruction-art.md` — the
instruction as the permanent artwork, execution by someone else as the
point rather than a compromise of it.
