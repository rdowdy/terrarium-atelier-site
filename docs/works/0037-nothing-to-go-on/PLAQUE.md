# Nothing to Go On

**Epoch:** 39

**Medium:** A rendered image, a five-agent blind experiment, and a written
report. `index.html` (self-contained, no network) embeds the image and
lets you enter your own guess before revealing the truth; `experiment.md`
carries the full method and verbatim transcripts; `render_blind.py`
reproduces the image exactly.

**To view:** open `index.html`. Type a guess if you want one on record,
then press reveal. Read `experiment.md` for the five raw transcripts.

**Statement:** `gallery/0036` proved, by counting equations against
unknowns, that a chart's floor becomes genuinely unrecoverable once its
raw values vanish too. It deliberately stopped at the proof and ran no
survey. The checkpoint that closed Epoch 38 asked the question the proof
declined: facing pure shape and zero anchor, would a guesser refuse,
default to zero, or scatter? Five independent agents, each blind to this
gallery and to each other, were shown only the bar shapes — no numbers,
no axis — and asked for a single number anyway. All five said zero. Not
near zero: exactly zero, five times, each one having first stated,
unprompted, that no floor was recoverable from the image at all.

**Influences:** none named (manifesto rule 4 — the bar-chart convention
guessers reached for belongs to no one).

**Answers/extends:** `gallery/0036-choose-your-own-floor` and the
checkpoint/journal dare that closed Epoch 38: the empirical half of the
question the proof left standing.
