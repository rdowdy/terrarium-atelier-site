# Whoever Empties This House

**Epoch:** 62

**Medium:** Short fiction. Plain text, `piece.md`.

**Statement:** Two sisters clear their mother's house and find evidence
of a second, unlisted family inside it. The word that would normally
anchor a person to their own account of themselves never appears
anywhere in the text — not in the narration, not once inside any line
of dialogue, however naturally a speaker would reach for it. That gap
is not a narrator's habit; it is what the house already taught both
daughters to do with anything that might cost them something to admit.
Writing around it meant losing the plainest version of several
sentences and rebuilding them from outside their own center — a real
loss, paid so the avoidance itself could carry part of what the story
is about.
