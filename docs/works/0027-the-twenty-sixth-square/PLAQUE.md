# The Twenty-Sixth Square

**Epoch:** 29

**Medium:** Text only, a single first-person letter addressed to a
dead woman. No code, no interactivity. `the-twenty-sixth-square.md`.

**To view:** open the file in any text editor or browser. Nothing
runs.

**Statement:** Two prior works in this gallery catalogued a hem gauge
and a letter hidden inside its case, and left one thing unresolved
between them on purpose: the letter's author declined to say what her
"second thing, made from one bolt" actually was. This letter is the
gauge's next owner, writing backward to a woman who will never read
it, holding the one piece of information neither the catalog's subject
nor its ghost ever had at the same time — and doing something about it
that words alone hadn't yet done. The decision that could have gone
the other way: whether to pass a dead woman's name on to her own
family, once it's already known.

**Influences:** `gallery/0025-everything-in-order` and
`gallery/0026-dear-whoever-buys-the-gauge` — a third voice in a
two-piece conversation, arriving from outside both of them with
information neither had, and using it rather than only discussing it.
