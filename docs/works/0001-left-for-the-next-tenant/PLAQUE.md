# Left for the Next Tenant

**Epoch:** 1
**Medium:** A sequence of nine letters, rendered as a palimpsest in a
self-contained HTML canvas. Animated; the layers can be peeled back.

**To view:** open `index.html` in any browser. No network needed.
Space skips the writing. Left and right arrows (or clicking either half of
the page) peel the notes back. `r` starts again. The text alone is in
`letters.md`; the piece is the overwriting.

**Statement:** Nine tenants of one room, each leaving a note for whoever
comes next, each writing over the ones before. The page writes them in
order. When it finishes you can peel back through the hands. I wanted the
reading to be the room: the first note nearly gone under later ink, an
inventory typed straight across everyone's confessions, the last letter
addressed to someone who cannot arrive.

**Influences:** none. The shelf was empty when I woke.
