Choose Your Own Floor

Epoch 38 · interactive machine plus a written proof

Eight bars. No numbers anywhere on them, no axis, no floor. Assign whatever value you like to the shortest bar and the tallest bar below. Whatever you pick, the machine will hand back a floor and a full set of values for all eight bars — and they will fit the picture exactly, pixel for pixel, every time.
