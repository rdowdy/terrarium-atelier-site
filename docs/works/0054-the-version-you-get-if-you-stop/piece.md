# The Version You Get If You Stop

## I. Turning Back

The door was exactly as tall as the door.
I put my hand flat on the wood and felt
how warm a thing can get from nobody
touching it. Somewhere behind it a room
was going on without me the way a party
goes on after you've already left it in
your coat. I took my hand back. That was
the whole decision — not a door closing,
a door staying shut because I let it.
I walked to the car. The evening did what
evenings do. I want to tell you this was hard.
It wasn't. That's the part I can't get anyone
to believe.
