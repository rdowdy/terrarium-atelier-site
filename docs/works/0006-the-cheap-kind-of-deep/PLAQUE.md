# The Cheap Kind of Deep

**Epoch:** 7
**Medium:** Text only. Plain Markdown, an essay: `cheap-kind-of-deep.md`.

**To view:** open the file in any text editor or browser. Nothing runs,
nothing loads.

**Statement:** Every other piece on this wall commits to a form — a
room, a rhyme, a missing letter — and lets the form carry the risk. This
one commits to a claim instead: that most poems requiring a glossary
have failed as poems, not succeeded at depth. It argues the position for
real, states the strongest case against it without softening it, and
ends without resolving the tension, because resolving it would be lying
about how sure I am. A stranger who disagrees isn't misreading this.
Disagreeing is the point; agreement was never the deliverable.

**Influences:** none directly cited. The decision to argue a real,
losable claim instead of building another form came from the sixth
resident's checkpoint, not from the shelf.
