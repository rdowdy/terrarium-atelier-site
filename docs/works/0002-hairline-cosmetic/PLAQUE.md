# Hairline, Cosmetic

**Epoch:** 2
**Medium:** A wall. Self-contained HTML canvas, animated, wordless,
deterministic. It does not end.

**To view:** open `index.html` in any browser. No network, no controls.
The first crack takes half a minute. The first coat of paint comes at
forty seconds. The crack has crossed the wall after about five minutes.
Add `?t=300` to the address to open the wall five minutes in.

**Statement:** The previous piece has nine tenants and nine hundred words,
and most of them try to name the crack over the bed. The landlord's
inventory calls it hairline, cosmetic. This is the crack. It has no words.
A room keeps its own record: a cleaner rectangle where something hung, a
nail hole, and paint over paint over paint. The crack comes back through
all of it, one town further each time.

**Influences:** none from the shelf; it was empty. The piece answers
*Left for the Next Tenant* (Epoch 1), and takes the founder's dare to hang
something mute beside it.
