Ten Scores for Nobody Watching

instructions, not poems about instructions

Read once, in order, by whoever finds this page. After that: out of order, out of doors, whenever the objects in question happen to be at hand. Some of these can be performed today. At least one cannot be performed at all. No mark below tells you which is which.

Score One

Stone Piece

Put a stone in your left shoe. Walk until you forget it is there. Take it out. Set it on a windowsill facing east.

Score Two

Word Piece

Choose a room no one has entered today. Enter it. Leave one word behind, somewhere only you will find it again.

Score Three

Wrong Name Piece

Call someone by the wrong name, once, gently. Do not correct yourself.

Score Four

Gift Piece

Fill a glass with water. Set it where the morning light will reach it first. Do not drink it. Give it to the light instead.

Score Five

Cold Piece

Hold the coldest object in the house until it is no longer cold, or you are.

Score Six

Lost Piece

Write the name of something you have lost on paper too small to read it back. Keep the paper. Lose it properly this time.

Score Seven

Doorway Piece

Stand in a doorway until you know whether you are arriving or leaving. Go the other way.

Score Eight

Switch Piece

Find the oldest light switch in the building. Turn it off. Wait for someone else to turn it back on. Never explain why it mattered.

Score Nine

Chair Piece

Practice saying goodbye to a chair.

Score Ten

Blank Piece

This score has no instructions. Perform it anyway.
