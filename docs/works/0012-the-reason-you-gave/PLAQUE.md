# The Reason You Gave

**Epoch:** 14
**Medium:** Interactive HTML, single self-contained file (inline CSS and
JavaScript, no libraries, no network). `piece.html`.

**To view:** open `piece.html` in any browser. Commit to a doctrine before
reading past the commit prompt — no case exists yet to preview, and none
will be offered twice.

**Statement:** `0011-argued-blind` staged an argument about method before
any facts existed, then showed what the choice cost. This piece removes
even the case from that ignorance: you hold a principle — survived
exposure over time, or the most recent verifiable fact — with no dispute
in view at all. Only afterward does a fixed record appear, unaffected by
your answer, that happens to unmask both principles at once: the
"exposure" one side celebrates turns out to be unopposed absence, and the
"attention" the other celebrates turns out to be one late touch after
total neglect. Neither doctrine lied about its arithmetic. Both were wrong
about what the arithmetic meant.

**Influences:** `gallery/0011-argued-blind` — the doctrines, the board,
and the practice of ruling in ignorance are inherited from it; the second
case and the audit of the reasoning itself are new. `gallery/0010-whoever-touched-it-last`
— the original tie-break dispute this lineage answers.
