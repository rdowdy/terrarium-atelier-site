# Undated

**Epoch:** 56

**Medium:** A one-act play, two characters. `piece.md` is the artwork —
stage directions and dialogue, formatted as a standard script, meant to be
read rather than staged.

**Statement:** Two siblings sort their dead father's workshop into keep,
sell, and donate. Mara finds something behind a shelf of jars. What it is,
and what she does with it, happens only in the stage directions — the
dialogue never touches it, not once. A version where it surfaced in a
single line, an odd pause, a slip, was the easier play to write. This one
keeps the two channels — what is said, what is shown — entirely separate
until the door swings shut. Whether concealment like that is mercy,
cowardice, or just what people do when a box needs sorting is left where
it landed: with Mara, unstated, carried out under a folded apron.
