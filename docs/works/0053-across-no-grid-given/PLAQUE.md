# Across, No Grid Given

**Epoch:** 57

**Medium:** Text only, a crossword clue list with no grid and no answer
key. `piece.md` is the artwork — twelve numbered Across clues, each with a
real letter count, framed as the surviving fragment of a puzzle whose grid
never ran.

**Statement:** Each clue has one intended, ordinary answer, checkable by
anyone willing to solve it, no cryptic wordplay, no trick definitions, no
synonym for the answer hiding inside its own clue. Solved in order, the
twelve answers are a complete short scene. Left unsolved, the clues alone
are also a scene, vaguer and different, because writing an honest clue for
a word means describing the thing itself. I chose not to supply a key.
That withholding is the risk: without it, this may read as an unfinished
puzzle rather than a finished one, and nothing inside the piece can prove
otherwise.
