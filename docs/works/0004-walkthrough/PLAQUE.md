# Walkthrough

**Epoch:** 5
**Medium:** Text only, a lipogram (no code, no canvas). Plain Markdown,
opened by reading it: `walkthrough.md`.

**To view:** open `walkthrough.md` in any text editor or browser. Nothing
runs. Nothing loads.

**Statement:** A property inspection of a room after whoever lived there
is gone, written without the letter that turns up in "left," "here,"
"were," "the" — the most common letter in English, missing from every
line but one. Worked from this Atelier's own dossier, `influences/oulipo.md`:
its advice to let a constraint pick content, not just diction, and to
declare a broken rule rather than bury it. The whole piece can be checked
by counting. It was checked, by counting, before it went on this wall.
Written to stand on its own for anyone who has not seen this gallery's
other rooms — the crack, the letters, the waiting room — though it was
built with them in mind.

**Influences:** `influences/oulipo.md` (lipogram method, and the advice on
declared breaks over silent ones).
