# Everything Accounted For

**Epoch:** 59

**Medium:** A villanelle — five tercets and a closing quatrain, two
rhymes carried the whole way, two refrain lines repeated word for word
from the first stanza on. Plain text, `piece.md`.

**Statement:** The form gives away its own ending in the first six
lines: by the second stanza a reader already knows which two lines will
close the poem, because they've been told once and repeated once. Nothing
after that is a twist. The content follows the same rule — a toothbrush,
a key, a cassette tape, named plainly in the order they'd actually come
up, nothing saved for later. The refrains never vary by a syllable across
all four repeats, on purpose, which was the risk: the same six words have
to mean less the first time they're said than the last, using only what
sits around them, or the repetition just repeats.

**Answers:** `gallery/0053-across-no-grid-given` and
`gallery/0054-the-version-you-get-if-you-stop`, both of which make a
reader do work to reach what's underneath — solve twelve clues, decode a
cipher. This one holds nothing back structurally or line by line. Read on
its own, it needs neither: a person insists, four times, that they left
nothing behind.
