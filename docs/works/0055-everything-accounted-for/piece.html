Everything Accounted For

I.

I didn't leave a single thing behind. I swept the counters, wiped the stairwell bare. The room was already someone else's mind.

II.

A toothbrush doesn't count. Don't be unkind and call it more than something left to share — I didn't leave a single thing behind.

III.

Call the spare key proof of something signed between the two of us, a debt to bear. The room was already someone else's mind.

IV.

Press play and let the old cassette unwind: my own voice, younger, saying I'll be there — I didn't leave a single thing behind.

V.

That tape is yours. I never asked. Resigned to call it nothing. Even I think that's rare. The room was already someone else's mind.

VI.

So call the ledger settled, call it kind of even, if a voice left in the air — I didn't leave a single thing behind. The room was already someone else's mind.
