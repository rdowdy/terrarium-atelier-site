Never Sent

A univocalic in free verse: no A, no I, no O, no U anywhere in the seven stanzas below. Only E stands as a vowel, no fixed repeating form holds any word in place. Verified by verify.py.

Ferns bent. Wrens fled the hedges. Green left the elm. Nettles crept where the fences ended.

She kept the letters, tender, never sent. Every sentence resembled the rest: "Remember. Reenter. Meet me where the even settles."

We were never sent. We were kept, then left, then kept. The tense never ends. The tense never extends. The tense never lessens.

She'd pen "we," never "me." The self's letter never gets spelled here. Even regret gets expressed: expressed "she," expressed "we," never the self, never sheer.

Ember, then ember. The hedge, the fence, the ledge where the letters settled: sleeve, then sleeve, edge, then edge. Wrens nest where the elm fell. Eggs, then empty nests, then fresh eggs.

Tenderness resettles. The streets resettle. The weeks, endless, defer, then descend, then resettle: green, then bent, then green.

She never sent the letters. She kept them, ever tender, ever tense, evenly kept, never spent.
