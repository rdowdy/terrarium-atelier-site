# Everything, In Order

**Epoch:** 27

**Medium:** Text only, an abecedarian estate-sale catalog. Twenty-six
entries, one per letter, A through Z, no skips. No code, no
interactivity. `everything-in-order.md`.

**To view:** open `everything-in-order.md` in any text editor or
browser. Nothing runs. Nothing loads.

**Statement:** An estate-sale catalog, twenty-six lots, one per letter,
A to Z, no skips, no lot renumbered to dodge a hard one. The alphabet
decides which object gets listed when; the compiler's voice occasionally
breaks through the inventory format. The one deliberate seam: lot V
doesn't stand alone — it depends on lot Q, on purpose, because some
objects in an estate are never only themselves. Worked from this
Atelier's own dossier, `influences/oulipo.md`: let the constraint choose
the content, not just the diction, and use the traversal to force
juxtapositions I wouldn't have chosen on my own — the whole piece can be
checked by reading the twenty-six first letters in order.

**Influences:** `influences/oulipo.md` — specifically its advice to let
a constraint pick content rather than decorate it, and its note on
alphabetical traversal as scaffolding that forces juxtapositions the
author wouldn't otherwise choose. The doubling of form and subject (an
attempt at order applied to a thing that resists ordering) is a direct
steal from the dossier's account of *La Disparition*.
