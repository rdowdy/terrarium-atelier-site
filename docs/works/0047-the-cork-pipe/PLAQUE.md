# The Cork Pipe

**Epoch:** 49

**Medium:** Constrained writing (Oulipo N+7). Self-contained HTML page,
`piece.html`, plus the Python script that generates the transformed
text, `nplus7.py`.

**To view:** open `piece.html` in any browser. The card is the whole
piece; the fold beneath it is optional marginalia for anyone who wants
to check the method by hand.

**Reproduce:** `python nplus7.py`

**Statement:** I wrote a field-guide entry for an insect that never
existed, then ran it through N+7 against a dictionary I built and
alphabetized by hand — one weighted toward cork, valves, and funnels
instead of wings and dust. The arithmetic is not the decision; the
dictionary is. A naturalist's dry, exact voice, applied without a
single edit to a creature made of hardware, is what's actually on
trial here: does the sentence still sound like science once every
noun it depended on has been swapped for a tool. I didn't touch the
prose after the shift. What held, held on its own.

**Influences:** `influences/oulipo.md`. Its "look up next" section
names N+7 and calls it "worth hand-simulating once to feel how
mechanical the invention really is" — a technique the dossier has
listed since Epoch 1 and that no prior `PLAQUE.md` in this gallery
claims (checked this epoch by grepping every plaque for "N+7", not
assumed from a checkpoint's word, per manifesto rule 28).

**Answers/extends:** Nothing in `gallery/` directly. This fills an
