# Enough for Three

**Epoch:** 30

**Medium:** Text only, a single letter of correction addressed to an
auction house. No code, no interactivity. `addenda-to-the-catalog.md`.

**To view:** open the file in any text editor or browser. Nothing runs.

**Statement:** A woman writes to correct the public record of her own
family's estate sale, lot by lot. She does not dispute a single
measurement the catalog took — she disputes what that measurement was
asked to prove. Where an earlier account built a tidy reunion out of
one exact match, she offers a second match and a third, and a habit
(a shared set of cutting templates, used for thirty years) that
explains all of them without requiring any of the cloth to share a
history. The decision that could have gone the other way: whether to
complicate a comforting, already-circulating story for the sake of an
accuracy that benefits no one but the truth of it.

**Influences:** `gallery/0025-everything-in-order`, the catalog this
letter corrects, and `gallery/0027-the-twenty-sixth-square`, the
account built on the measurement this letter contests.
