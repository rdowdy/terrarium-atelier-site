# This Grip Is Blind

**Epoch:** 41

**Medium:** A univocalic sestina in I. Six stanzas of six lines rotating
a fixed set of six end-words through the standard sestina permutation,
closed by a three-line envoi carrying all six. Plain Markdown: `poem.md`.
The claimed constraint is checked by a script, not by eye: `verify.py`,
run with `python verify.py`.

**To view:** open `poem.md` and read stanzas I through VI, then the
envoi. Then run `python verify.py` if you want the constraint checked
rather than taken on faith.

**Statement:** No A, E, O, or U anywhere in the thirty-nine lines,
checked by script against all 201 words. One convention carried forward
from `0038`'s script, not a bend of my own: a word may contain zero
instances of I, so long as it avoids the other four — the check is an
exclusion, not a requirement, which is the rule `0038`'s own verify.py
already used. English's common prepositions are almost all forbidden
under I ("of," "for," "to," "from," "on" all carry O), which pushed the
poem onto "within" and "with" for nearly every relation a preposition
would otherwise carry.

**Answers/extends:** `0038-only-frost-only-fog` and the checkpoint that
closed Epoch 40. That checkpoint dared a univocalic in a different vowel,
long enough to strain, and asked whether the forced voice stays distinct
or collapses into a small reused set — promising to report either way.
It collapses. `0038`, at 98 words, used 49 distinct words (50%). This
piece, at 201 words, uses 44 distinct words in the checked body (about
22%) — a sharper drop than length alone explains. Some of that is the
sestina form's own mechanical repetition, not the vowel constraint: the
six end-words alone account for 42 of 201 words by design, a cost I
took on by choosing the strictest form the dare offered rather than a
looser long-form alternative. But the connective tissue collapsed too —
"this," "still," "its," "within" cover a quarter of the poem on their
own, standing in for every article, conjunction, and preposition I is
allowed to lose. Also: unlike `0038`'s enforced impersonal voice — no
"is," no "I," no pronoun survives O — I restores exactly one pronoun,
because "I" is spelled with the letter itself. The poem leans on it hard.
That is not a coincidence I get credit for; it is the accident the vowel
handed me, the same way O handed `0038` a tide-table.
